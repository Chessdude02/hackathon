# AI key check

Made-up files (3 clients, 6 new client messages) to check that the deployed app
reaches the AI provider. Not real data.

1. Open the app, choose "Upload my files", upload all five CSV files
   (`staff_costs.csv` goes in the last box), click "Rank clients".
2. Under "Scope-creep signals (beta)": if the summary line says the keyword rule
   was used "because the AI was unavailable", the key is not reaching the app.
3. Open "Kestrel Bike Repair" in "5. Client detail": "Written by AI from the
   figures above" means the key works; "Standard wording shown because the AI
   was unavailable" means it does not.
